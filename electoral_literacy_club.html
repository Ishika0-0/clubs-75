Electoral Literacy Club JEC

"Empowering Tomorrow's Voters: The Electoral Literacy Club (ELC) The Electoral Literacy Club (ELC) at our institution is a dynamic platform designed to engage students in meaningful activities and hands-on experiences that educate and empower them about their electoral rights and responsibilities. Our Mission: At the heart of the ELC lies a mission to foster a culture of active citizenship and electoral participation among the youth. Through innovative and interactive initiatives, we aim to: Educate and Sensitize, Engage Through Activities, Strengthen Electoral Participation,

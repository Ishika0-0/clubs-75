JEC Student Committee

Welcome to the JEC Student Committee, the heartbeat of student life at Jabalpur Engineering College (JEC). As the premier student-led organization on campus, we are dedicated to enhancing the overall student experience and fostering a vibrant community of learning, growth, and innovation. At JEC Student Committee, we believe in the power of student leadership and collaboration to drive positive change and enrich the lives of our fellow students. Our committee comprises passionate and dedicated individuals who are committed to serving the student body and making a difference in the JEC community. Through a wide range of activities, events, and initiatives, we strive to cater to the diverse interests and needs of our students. From organizing cultural festivals, technical competitions, and sports tournaments to hosting workshops, seminars, and guest lectures, we aim to provide opportunities for students to explore their passions, develop new skills, and create lasting memories.

Automotive Society of JEC

We are a dynamic and forward-thinking community of automotive enthusiasts, industry professionals, and students passionate about the ever-evolving world of automobiles. Our mission is to foster knowledge exchange, innovation, and camaraderie among like-minded individuals who share a common love for cars and automotive technology. Our society prides itself on being a hub for automotive excellence, providing a platform that brings together diverse perspectives from automotive engineers, designers, researchers, and students alike. Through engaging events, workshops, and knowledge-sharing sessions, we aim to stay at the forefront of industry trends, advancements, and best practices. Whether you're a seasoned veteran with years of experience in the automotive industry or a budding enthusiast eager to learn more, we have something to offer for everyone. Our events cover a wide range of topics, from cutting-edge technology developments, sustainable mobility solutions, electric vehicles, autonomous driving, to classic car restoration and everything in between.

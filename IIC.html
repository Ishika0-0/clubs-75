Institution's Innovation Council JEC


The "Institute's Innovation Council JEC" is a dynamic community or club within our college dedicated to fostering innovation, creativity, and entrepreneurial spirit among its members. It serves as a platform for students to collaborate, ideate, and implement innovative solutions to real-world challenges.
the Institute's Innovation Council JEC serves as a vibrant hub for nurturing innovative thinking, fostering collaboration, and empowering students to become change-makers in their fields. It embodies the spirit of creativity, experimentation, and forward-thinking, contributing to the holistic development of its members and the college community as a whole.

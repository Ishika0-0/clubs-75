KAARWAAN

Kaarwaa.N... is a non-government orgranisation founded by Jabalpur Engineering College (JEC), Jabalpur students who are committed to work for poor, underprivileged, downtrodden and unaware, with the primary aim to teach students for JNV(Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya) Entrance Exam and promote ‘girls education’. Through different activities we try to address different issues like education, health and hygiene, environmental issues, government scheme and plans, livelihood and community issues. It is our empathy, the realisation that being citizens of this country, it is our responsibility to take the step for the welfare of another India.

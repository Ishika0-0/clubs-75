JEC's Women Society

The JEC Women's Society is dedicated to empowering women in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) fields. Our mission is to provide support and resources to help girls pursue diverse careers in STEM and break through societal stereotypes. We believe in the potential of every woman to excel in STEM disciplines and aim to create an environment that fosters their growth and success.

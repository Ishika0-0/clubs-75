Ankuram

Welcome to Ankuram, where the spirit of entrepreneurship ignites and innovation takes center stage! Ankuram is not just an event; it's a one-day carnival designed to kickstart your entrepreneurial journey. It all begins with the grand inauguration of the E-cell, the heartbeat of innovation at our institution. From there, get ready for a day packed with excitement, learning, and fun. Experience the thrill of fast-paced games that challenge your agility and strategic thinking. Test your knowledge and wit in fun and engaging quizzes that will leave you both entertained and enlightened. Explore the knowledgeable stalls of startups, where you'll discover groundbreaking ideas and interact with the minds behind them.

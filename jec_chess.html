Jec Chess Club

JEC Chess Club: Cultivating Minds, Forging Strategies The JEC Chess Club stands as a bastion of strategic thinking and intellectual camaraderie within Jabalpur Engineering College (JEC).Our Mission: At the JEC Chess Club, our mission is twofold: to nurture chess enthusiasts and to elevate the game's status as a respected discipline within our academic community. Through a combination of regular practice sessions, competitive tournaments, and educational workshops.

Whether you're a seasoned chess player or a novice eager to learn, the JEC Chess Club welcomes you with open arms. Join us to explore the depths of this timeless game, forge lasting friendships, and become part of a vibrant chess community at Jabalpur Engineering College.

   "Struggle of pawn leads to queen one day, so just stay focused and play chess."

Dance On JEC

""Dance On" is a vibrant dance studio and club within JEC dedicated to showcasing the diverse and exceptional dance talent of our students. Our platform serves as a hub for students who are passionate about dance to come together, express themselves creatively, and hone their skills in various dance styles.
